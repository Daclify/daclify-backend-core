# Customer-funded account creation

The user requires each customer payment to cover actual RAM and CPU/NET provisioning before a seller receives proceeds. The confirmed third-party commission is 5% of the full advertised sale price, not the amount after costs. The configured rate remains authoritative. For a 10-unit purchase with 2 units of costs and a 5% rate, provisioning receives 2, the DAO receives 0.5 commission and the seller receives 7.5.

Native TLOS arrives before provisioning. Measure the actual provisioning balance decrease before any payout, then pay the configured commission and seller remainder. Customer payment must cover both costs and commission; failure rolls back payment, account creation, resource actions, inventory updates and receipts. Existing float must not subsidize a completed native purchase. First-party native sales retain resources from the payment too; the DAO receives the remaining first-party proceeds, and the existing $1 net-profit requirement remains enforced.

Preserve existing serialized sales, configuration, tiers, listings, policy and action layouts. Reuse the transient profit guard for all settlement stages, keep it until payouts finish, and add a versioned provisioning receipt alongside legacy sales. Reject inbound deposits during settlement so callbacks cannot conceal costs. Replays, unauthorized finalization and failed payouts must not duplicate or partially commit accounting.

First-party card payments still require working-capital TLOS because the payment provider collects fiat. Captured verified net fiat must cover provisioning and the required margin; record that cost allocation. No fiat-to-TLOS conversion mechanism is configured, so do not invent automatic reserve replenishment. Third-party card sales remain blocked at API and contract boundaries until seller merchant routing exists. Gross-only legacy first-party settlement remains restricted to its existing trusted settler and may not provision at a loss.

Keep the current dynamic normal-account floor, resource package, native conversion premium, sale increments, destinations and seller creation permission. Seller forms and purchaser copy explain cost recovery plus commission on the full price. No provider charge, seller permission mutation, reserve transfer or production release is included. Testnet code deployment and dev publication follow the ongoing authorized workflow after verification.
