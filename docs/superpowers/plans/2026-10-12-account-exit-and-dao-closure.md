# Account Exit and DAO Closure Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use `superpowers:executing-plans` inline. No delegation. Track each checkbox and actual evidence in the master ledger.

**Goal:** Let a user leave one/all DAOs and delete their Daclify user, offering sole-member DAO closure while preserving claims, continuity and truthful cleanup status.

**Architecture:** New native departure/retirement actions preserve existing member/DAO serialization and financial records. A PostgreSQL leased workflow coordinates signed native operations, hosted personal-data cleanup and independent-app notices. Frontend confirmation separates leaving, closing and hosted-account erasure instead of treating them as one transaction.

**Tech Stack:** Antelope C++/VERT/native fixtures, TypeScript/Fastify/PostgreSQL jobs, canonical SDK/routes, Vue and Playwright.

**Spec:** [Design](../specs/2026-10-12-self-service-mini-apps-and-account-exit-design.md); [master](2026-10-12-self-service-mini-apps-and-account-exit.md); [account continuity](../specs/2026-10-12-account-access-continuity-design.md).

## Global Constraints

Honor master constraints. Ordinary members sign their own departure. Keep financial claims/stake and once-only settlement; no implicit transfer, burn, donation or provider cancellation. Sole-member closure is offered, never silently performed. Hosted data erasure may complete while a native exit remains unresolved. Missing successors or funds do not justify retaining all account fields indefinitely. Preserve canonical/retired account ownership and actual private-history recovery.

## Review Focus

1. Final administrator/controller departs with outstanding liabilities — X1 rejects unsafe closure, preserves settlement rights.
2. An erasure worker crashes after a chain submit or loses its lease — X2 reconciles before retry.
3. A retired account alias or old backup resurrects login/profile access — X2 handles every owned alias and restore exclusions.
4. A user deletes hosted credentials before receiving a usable exit/claim report — X3 preserves narrowly scoped status/settlement access.
5. A disconnected mini app claims cleanup or replays another app's notice — X4 verifies scope and reports evidence limits.
