# Discover and list community mini apps

A directory entry advertises an app. **Maintainer verified** means an authenticated account proved control of the exact HTTPS origin with a short-lived challenge. It does not certify the app, connect a DAO, grant a native permission or approve its privacy policy.

Sign in to Mini apps → Discover, submit plain-text details, request verification and publish the returned JSON at the exact proof URL. The verifier pins a public DNS address, uses TLS, refuses redirects/private addresses and limits both time and response size. Complete the ten-minute challenge once. Editing the origin invalidates proof and publication; moderated hiding cannot be undone by an owner edit or another proof.

Moderation uses current active administrators of the native governing/platform DAO identified by existing platform status. Publication stays unavailable when that governing context is unconfigured. No founder approval queue is needed. A moderator may hide or restore a verified listing. Communities may submit bounded reports; reports expire after thirty days. The service caps eight listings per owner, ten thousand total, one proof/transfer per listing and one report per account/listing with finite submission/report budgets. Every write checks a current active account and CSRF; transfer/moderation also require fresh body-bound account control.

Withdrawal removes the entry from browsing immediately; bounded cleanup deletes its children and then its row. An owner can edit, withdraw or offer transfer to a specified active successor account. The successor proves control with a fresh challenge bound to its own account and listing within ten minutes. The transfer is atomic; the previous owner then loses edit rights. Listing renewal retains a ninety-day maximum lifetime. Bounded cleanup removes expired proof/transfer/report records before expired listings. Account erasure removes owned entries and unnecessary linked evidence; a completed transfer preserves the successor's entry. These are controlled-service actions; blockchain history and independent app data remain separate.

Public text is escaped by the frontend. External HTTPS documentation/source/app links open with isolation and no remote executable content is loaded into Daclify. Review the app's database, retention and permissions before connecting it through a DAO administrator and normal member consent. Database hosting remains a future service.
