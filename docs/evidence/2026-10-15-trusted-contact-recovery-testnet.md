# Trusted-contact recovery on shared testnet — 15 October 2026

Trusted-contact recovery is deployed at [Account → Trusted-contact recovery](https://testnet.app.daclify.com/account). The public API advertises `trusted-contact-recovery-v1`. Members choose three contacts in advance; all accept, and two approve the same receiving key before a 48-hour wait. An existing controller can cancel. Account recovery preserves the DAO member identity and financial records; external wallet private keys and funds are separate.

The installed cohort is core/SDK **0.15.0-alpha.30**, modules **0.10.0-alpha.40**, frontend **0.15.0-alpha.41**, and Community MCP **0.1.0-alpha.20**. The immutable source snapshot records core `fcff2097e9b8fc52a2da28ed04cfdddc626bbecc`, modules `cec1e3fe788fc89d49195fa5507771d9e8692dcb`, and frontend `f1811a1ffdfc47064450b6814bb105c843206b00`. This is the existing development deployment from `dev`; mainnet and registry publication were not performed.

Document recovery remains **off by default for every DAO**. Deployment did not enroll contacts, alter a DAO's privacy policy or create document backups. A DAO must permit contact recovery and the member must back up eligible epoch keys before loss. As agreed, a sufficient contact quorum can also decrypt those backed-up keys without the member. Later revocation cannot erase copies already held by contacts.
