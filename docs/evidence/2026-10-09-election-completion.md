# Maximum-seat native completion and historical election adoption

The owned native Spring fixture passed **2/2 cases, none skipped, in 158.90 seconds**. Both cases create an eight-seat election with the maximum 80-byte title, eight distinct internal signing identities and eight tied votes. They tighten both runtime and Decide to their exact occupied native RAM before finalization. All eight terms are issued once, without giving members additional permanent roles. Replay rejects with `BALLOT_FINALIZED` and preserves terms, member state and native usage.

The new-contract case allocates its physical term hold when voting starts. The historical case creates and votes with the actual older core/Decide binaries, then upgrades with the observer rebind and source/installed-code updates. Before adoption, `checkquota` refuses with `RAM_ELECTION_HOLD_REQUIRED`. Bounded `adoptelect` creates the missing hold without changing original members, elections or ballots. A completed adoption retry changes neither hold nor counters.

Both physical limits are checked after tightening and restored after the test. The result qualifies the tested finite completion path, not unlimited future writes or a public deployment.
