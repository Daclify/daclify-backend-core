# Password-free devices and original-key recovery — 2026-10-10

Implemented inline under the approved [design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-passwordless-devices-and-vault-recovery.md) and [plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-passwordless-devices-and-vault-recovery.md). No delegation, main/production release, registry publishing, chain authority changes or asset transfers were performed.

The feature preserves account IDs and both original keys, starts each pairing as sign-in-only, and lets users choose wallet-protected, passkey-protected or explicitly consented Daclify-assisted full access. Fresh verified logins produce one-use session/account/credential/origin-bound grants. Existing-device approval encrypts the original keys to a fresh P-256 recipient. Recovered keys stay in browser memory; existing JSON/password/code fallbacks remain usable.

Assisted consent is durable before any original unlocking material is delivered. Failed storage cannot roll back that authority disclosure. Historical strict-private DAO memberships and pending strict creation block assisted setup; managed/assisted identities are reported in account/join/creation flows. Restored sessions/ceremonies are revoked and full-access methods quarantined until current ownership/revocation and permanent assisted-authority history is reconciled. Original-key possession cannot reconstruct missing authority history.

Actual independent host credentials, off-site destination/retention/current history and real wallet/authenticator/PRF matrix checks remain unavailable. Hosted/private-client modes must remain off until their actual reports pass. Manual probes use an isolated staging harness with disposable accounts and injected test configuration; do not fabricate production qualification to bootstrap testing.

No live assisted-email/Telegram key-service deployment, physically independent restore or hardware PRF/wallet qualification was claimed.
